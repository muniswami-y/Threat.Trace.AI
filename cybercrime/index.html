Department Responsibilities & Powers
The Head of the Cyber Crime Department oversees statewide cyber crime investigations, digital forensics analysis, officer deployment, and inter-agency coordination under the IT Act.
Authorizes official notices to banks, ISPs, and telecom operators
Issues immediate bank account freeze orders to prevent fraud losses
Certifies digital and electronic forensic evidence
Supervises special cyber crime task forces and complex cases
