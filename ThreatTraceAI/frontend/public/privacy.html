1. Introduction

Threat Trace AI ("we", "our", or "the Extension") provides advanced email forensics, phishing heuristic detection, threat attribution, and incident dispatching. This Privacy Policy details our commitment to zero-log ephemeral scanning and user privacy.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Threat Trace AI operates under a Zero-Trust and Minimal-Data paradigm. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

  • Email Forensics Data: Technical headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, Received chain), URLs, sender domains, and extracted indicators (IP addresses, phone numbers) are processed solely to compute threat risk scores.
  • No Continuous Private Message Storage: Regular email content is processed ephemeral in-memory without persistent retention.
  • Explicit Cybercrime Incident Reporting: Only when you explicitly click "Report Incident to Cybercrime", the compiled forensic summary, evidence logs, and case payload are transmitted to secure law enforcement / administrative databases.

3. Browser Permissions & Justification

  • activeTab & scripting: Required to inspect active Gmail tabs and display in-page forensic indicators without reading background browsing history.
  • storage: Used to store local user preferences and telemetry cache on your device.
  • host_permissions (mail.google.com, vercel.app, onrender.com): Necessary to attach the forensic scanner overlay in Gmail and communicate with the ThreatTrace verification API.
